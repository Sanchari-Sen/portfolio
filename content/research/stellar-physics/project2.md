### Key Investigations

* **Solar Dynamo Mechanics:** Investigated the fundamental physics behind solar magnetic field variations, tracking surface sunspot formation mechanics alongside global magnetic pole-flip cyclic phases.
* **Time-Series Forecasting:** Built predictive machine learning models targeting monthly sunspot numbers, utilizing historical solar dataset baselines sourced directly from the World Data Center SILSO.
* **Architecture Comparison:** Evaluated performance margins across traditional statistical regression modeling approaches against multi-layered deep learning architectures optimized for sequential tracking.
