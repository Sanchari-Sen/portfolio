### Key Investigations

* **Cosmological Parameter Spaces:** Investigated the estimation of core parameters governing cosmic expansion dynamics—specifically the **Hubble constant ($H_0$)** and the **matter density parameter ($\Omega_m$)**—modeled within a foundational Newtonian cosmology framework.
* **Bayesian Statistical Architecture:** Implemented a robust parameter estimation pipeline utilizing a standard Gaussian likelihood function mapped alongside uniform (flat) prior distributions to represent initial parameter uncertainties.
* **Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) Sampling:** Utilized MCMC stochastic sampling techniques to efficiently traverse the multi-dimensional parameter space, generating posterior probability distributions and tracing parameter degeneracies.

---

### Outcomes & Directions

* Successfully derived numerical constraints for both $H_0$ and $\Omega_m$ that display strong statistical convergence and align cleanly with established precision observational datasets.
* Demonstrated the utility and mathematical robustness of Bayesian inference structures for breaking parameter degeneracies in astronomical model fitting.
