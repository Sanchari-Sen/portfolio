### Key Investigations
* **R-Matrix Formalism Optimization:** Applied the multi-level, multi-channel R-matrix framework to model resonant scattering behaviors, identifying and characterizing unstable energy levels in compound nuclei appearing as sharp cross-section anomalies.
* **Compound Nucleus Profiling:** Conducted comprehensive numerical modeling of the resonant states within the compound nucleus \\(^{16}\\text{O}\\) via the elastic channel \\(^{12}\\text{C}(\\alpha,\\alpha)^{12}\\text{C}\\).
* **Cross-Section Computation:** Computed fine-grid differential reaction cross sections mapped strictly against variations in center-of-mass excitation energies using the C++ based **AZURE2** configuration platform.
